This passage serves as the introduction and opening of Alessandro Manzoni's *I Promessi Sposi*. The narrator explains that he has discovered an old, badly written manuscript from the 17th century and decided to rewrite its story in clearer, more modern Italian. The tale is set in 1628 in the territory of Lecco, near Lake Como, and centers on a local curate, Don Abbondio, who, while walking home one evening, encounters two armed thugs known as *bravi*. After a lengthy digression on the history and persistence of these hired ruffians despite repeated government bans, the narrator reveals that the bravi are waiting specifically for Don Abbondio. They confront him and, on behalf of an unnamed powerful lord, order him not to perform the upcoming marriage of Renzo Tramaglino and Lucia Mondella—threatening him if he disobeys.
